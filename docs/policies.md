# Policies

## Cancellations and refunds

- Cancel 24 hours or more before your tour for a full refund.
- Cancel less than 24 hours before for a credit toward any future tour within 12 months.
- No-shows are not refunded.
- If we cancel for weather, you choose a full refund or a free reschedule.

## Late arrivals

Tours leave on time. If you arrive after departure we will try to call you and
tell you where to catch up, but we cannot hold the group. Missed tours are
treated as no-shows.

## Accessibility

- The Candlelight Ghost Walk is wheelchair and stroller accessible.
- Service animals are welcome on every tour.
- Large-print tour notes are available on request at the office.
- Tell us about any needs when you book and we will do our best.

## Photography and recording

Photos are welcome on all tours. Video recording of the guide is not permitted
without asking the guide first.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are sold at the office and online in any amount from $12 to $300.
They never expire.

## Privacy

We keep your name, email, and phone only to run your booking and to notify you
of cancellations. We do not sell or share guest information.
