# About Gettysburg Lantern Tours

Gettysburg Lantern Tours is a fictional family-run walking tour company used as
sample data for this project. Any resemblance to a real business is coincidental.

- Founded 2014 by Dana and Marcus Reilly.
- Office and meeting point: 412 Baltimore Street, Gettysburg, PA 17325 (the green
  door between the candle shop and the bookstore).
- Phone: (717) 555-0142. Email: hello@gettysburglanterntours.example
- Season: March 1 through November 30. Closed December through February except
  for private bookings.
- Guides: eight licensed guides. All ghost tour guides are also trained in
  Gettysburg battlefield history, so every tour mixes documented history with
  local legend.
- Group size: maximum 25 guests per guide on public tours.
