# Frequently Asked Questions

## Booking

**Do I need to book ahead?**
Yes for Friday and Saturday, and for all of October. Walk-ups are usually fine
Sunday through Thursday outside October. Book online or call (717) 555-0142.

**Can I book for a group of 30?**
Public tours cap at 25 per guide. For 26 or more we run a private tour with two
guides. Call to arrange.

**Do you take credit cards?**
Yes, all major cards online and at the office. Cash is also accepted at the office.

**Is there a military or senior discount?**
Active military, veterans, and guests 65 and over get $3 off adult tickets.
Show ID at the office.

## Getting there

**Where do I park?**
Free street parking on Baltimore Street after 6:00 pm. The Race Horse Alley
parking garage (two blocks north) is $1 per hour and never fills up.

**Where exactly do we meet?**
At the office, 412 Baltimore Street. Look for the green door and the lanterns
hanging outside. Arrive 15 minutes before your tour.

## The tours

**Is it actually scary?**
The Candlelight Ghost Walk and Kids' Lantern Hunt are storytelling tours, not
haunted attractions. Nobody jumps out at you. The Hidden Cemetery Tour is darker
in tone and is adults only.

**How much walking is there?**
It depends on the tour. See the tours page for distance and terrain. The
Candlelight Ghost Walk is the easiest at one flat mile with three sitting breaks.

**Can I bring my dog?**
Well-behaved leashed dogs are welcome on the Candlelight Ghost Walk and the
Battlefield at Dusk tour. No dogs on the Hidden Cemetery Tour.

**What if it rains?**
Tours run in light rain. Bring an umbrella or poncho. In heavy rain or lightning
we cancel and offer a full refund or a free reschedule. We text and email
everyone by 5:00 pm if a tour is cancelled.

**Are the tours in English only?**
Yes. We do not currently offer tours in other languages.

**Can my kids come on the Hidden Cemetery Tour if I'm with them?**
No. The Hidden Cemetery Tour is strictly 18 and over, even with a parent.
