# Tours

All tours start at the office, 412 Baltimore Street. Arrive 15 minutes early.
Prices are per person. Children 5 and under are free on all tours.

| Tour | Length | Walking | Best for | Adult | Child (6–12) | Departs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Candlelight Ghost Walk | 75 min | 1 mile, flat sidewalks | Families, first visit | $18 | $12 | Nightly 8:00 pm, Apr–Oct; Fri–Sat only Mar and Nov |
| Battlefield at Dusk | 2 hours | 2.5 miles, some grass and gentle hills | History fans, teens and up | $28 | $18 | Fri–Sun 6:30 pm, Apr–Oct |
| Hidden Cemetery Tour | 90 min | 1.5 miles, uneven ground, one steep path | Adults, photographers | $24 | not offered | Thu–Sat 9:30 pm, May–Oct |
| Kids' Lantern Hunt | 45 min | half a mile, flat | Ages 4–10 with an adult | $12 | $12 | Sat 6:00 pm, Jun–Aug |
| Private Tour | 90 min | your choice of route | Groups, bachelorette parties, reunions | $300 flat for up to 12, $20 each additional | — | Any day, by arrangement |

## Which tour is right for you

- **Young kids (under 10):** Kids' Lantern Hunt in summer, otherwise the
  Candlelight Ghost Walk. The Hidden Cemetery Tour is adults only (18+).
- **Not much walking:** Candlelight Ghost Walk. It stays on flat sidewalks with
  three sitting breaks.
- **Strollers and wheelchairs:** Candlelight Ghost Walk is fully accessible.
  The other tours cross grass or uneven ground.
- **Serious history, fewer ghost stories:** Battlefield at Dusk is about 70 percent
  documented history, 30 percent legend.
- **Scariest:** Hidden Cemetery Tour. Not recommended for anyone under 18.
- **Photography:** Hidden Cemetery Tour includes two ten-minute photo stops.
  Tripods are allowed.

## What to bring

- Comfortable closed-toe shoes.
- A light jacket, even in summer. It gets cool after dark.
- Water. There are no restrooms on the route after the office.
- Lanterns are provided, one per party. Flashlights are fine too.
